When accessing this terminal interface, you may optionally share your company or college organization name in the initialization boot screen. This telemetry is collected exclusively to map demographic reach.

>> Data Fields: Organization name, Submission Timestamp, System-generated UUID.
>> Storage: Secure database hosted on local deployment node.

02 // CONTACT & COMMUNICATIONS

If you initiate contact via email (mailto link) or phone (tel link) displayed on the system console, your contact details (email address, phone number, name) will be processed solely to respond to your inquiry.

>> Access Scope: Direct responses for professional inquiries.
>> Processing: Kept strictly confidential, never sold, shared, or distributed.

03 // COOKIES & TRACKING

This system values clean execution. There are no advertising scripts, external marketing trackers, or third-party cookies active on this system.

>> Analytics: Standard server logs for load management and error troubleshooting.

04 // YOUR DATA RIGHTS

You retain full rights to request the removal of your organization metadata from the system logs. You can sever this connection at any time by requesting deletion via the contact options.
